AxyomCore has been named a finalist in the 2026 Glotel Awards by Telecoms.com for 5G Innovation of the Year for its cloud native 5G core and work with Telenor on Autonomous Edge. The Glotel Awards recognize innovation and excellence across the global telecommunications industry, highlighting the companies and technologies helping shape the future of telecom. AxyomCore’s selection recognizes a new approach to 5G network resilience that can help keep remote communities and critical services connected even when connectivity to the central network is disrupted.
