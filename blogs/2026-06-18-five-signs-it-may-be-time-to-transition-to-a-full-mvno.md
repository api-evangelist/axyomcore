The thin MVNO model may have taken you this far, but it may not get you where you need to go next. When operators first enter the market as MVNOs, or Mobile Virtual Network Operators, the light MVNO model often makes sense: lease network access from a host MNO, stay asset-light and move fast. For early growth, it works.
